TRUE SKY 🌌

A birth-chart app that starts with the astronomical sky: real planetary positions, real IAU constellation boundaries, the real local horizon — then adds a clearly labelled symbolic layer.

Your actual sky

Method: planetary positions come from Astronomy Engine. The constellation result uses its IAU constellation-boundary function, rather than a 12-sign zodiac. This is an astronomy layer; any personality meaning is a separate symbolic interpretation.

What makes True Sky different

🌌 Real constellationsUnequal IAU constellation regions, including Ophiuchus.
🕰 Exact momentHistorical local time is converted to UTC before calculating the sky.
🧭 Local horizonBirthplace affects what was rising, setting and above the horizon.
Prototype. Astronomy Engine is used for calculation; its constellation function maps J2000 RA/Dec to the official constellation regions. Symbolic interpretations are intentionally kept separate from astronomical facts.
